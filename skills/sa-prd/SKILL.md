description: "PRD of an epic (épico) in the Sabiá flow. After the P.O.'s decision to invest in pitch.md, writes prd.md in the epic folder straight to the base branch, proportional to size: problem, goals and metrics, actors, requirements (one block per requirement with type HF, REG, EL or GL and action inclui, altera or exclui, existing ids from ELM, provisional ids for new ones), non-goals, decisions and open questions. Facts come from sa-pesquisar, business rules from sa-entrevistar; nothing is invented. The P.O. (produto) approves in the document itself (\"Aprovado: <user> · <date>\" at the top), then it offers slicing (sa-refinar). Also changes an approved PRD in a new version, gets it re-approved and takes the change to the affected slices. No MR and no publishing: requirements reach DOORS through the slices. Use when the user says \"escrever o PRD do EP-03\", \"aprovar o PRD do EP-03\", \"retomar o PRD\", \"mudar o PRD do EP-03\"."
