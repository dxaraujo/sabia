description: "Skill for the Especificador (specifier) role in the Sabiá flow — drives the specification of an epic (épico, EP-nn) from the [ESP] task to the PRD GATE, and the [ESP] of a slice (fatia, FT-nnn): claims the [ESP] in EWM (the epic becomes iniciado on the base branch), sets up the local working folder outside git (.sabia/local/especificacao/), routes by the Fase in the epic's status.md (sa-entrevistar → sa-prd → PRD GATE), brings the approved PRD to the base branch, publishes the approved artifacts to ELM linked to the [ESP] and completes it (slicing comes right after and is the P.O.'s or SM's, sa-fatiar) and, at the end of each session with the client, records the elicitation in the [ESP] (comment + .md attachments with the latest version of each artifact) and logs the session's hours. The [ESP] of a slice writes the ELM artifacts its card lists as affected (include, change, exclude), approved by the specifier, recorded on the base branch and published to ELM linked to the [ESP] when it completes, which then offers fitting the slice into the open sprint, where the [DEV] is born. A slice that comes back to specification reopens its [ESP] in the current sprint; from a closed sprint, a new [ESP] <id> Revisão <n> is born. Each client meeting during the open [ESP] becomes an ATA (meeting minutes) written from its transcript (.vtt or .md in reunioes/, kept only on this machine), approved by the person and published to DOORS Next on approval, with its decisions recorded as D-nn. In a project with screens (Telas = sim), the PRD starts only after the client approves a prototype: each version (fictitious data only) is attached to the [ESP] as prototipo-vN, and the approval (who, date, version, evidence: ATA or e-mail) becomes a D-nn. Another machine resumes by downloading the attachments. Use when the user says \"especificar o EP-03\", \"pegar a [ESP] do EP-03\", \"especificar a FT-031\", \"pegar a [ESP] da FT-031\", \"retomar a especificação\", \"fechar a sessão de especificação\", \"tenho a transcrição da reunião\", \"registrar a ata\", \"tenho o protótipo\", \"o cliente aprovou o protótipo\"."
